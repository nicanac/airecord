# Product Context

## Why MeetFlow AI?

In the fast-paced enterprise environment, meetings are often productive but their value is lost immediately after they end. Documentation is tedious, action items are forgotten, and the "vibe" or sentiment of the meeting is never captured.

MeetFlow AI solves this by transforming the meeting experience from a passive "record and forget" model to an active "capture and act" model.

## Problems Solved
1.  **Lost Context:** "What was decided about the backend?" -> Solved by Semantic Search.
2.  **Forgotten Tasks:** "Who was supposed to email the client?" -> Solved by Action Item Extraction.
3.  **Invisible Sentiment:** "Did the team feel good about this roadmap?" -> Solved by Visual Sentiment Analysis.
4.  **Meeting Fatigue:** "Do we really need an hour for this?" -> Solved by Efficiency Metrics.

## How It Works
1.  **Capture:** User opens the app (Mobile/Web) and starts a session.
2.  **Process:** Audio is streamed to AI providers; text and sentiment are returned in real-time.
3.  **Review:** Post-meeting, the user sees a dashboard with highlights, action items, and a sentiment graph.
4.  **Act:** Tasks are pushed to project management tools.
